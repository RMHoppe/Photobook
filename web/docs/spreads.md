## What is a spread?

A **spread** is a pair of facing pages (left + right) shown together on the canvas. A book is made of multiple spreads in sequence. The first and last spreads may be single cover/endpaper pages if endpapers are enabled.

## Adding spreads

Click **+ Spread** in the footer, or press **N**. A new blank spread is inserted after the current one and becomes the active spread.

## Navigating spreads

| Method | Action |
|--------|--------|
| **← / →** buttons in footer | Previous / next spread |
| **← → ↑ ↓** arrow keys | Previous / next spread |
| Click a thumbnail | Jump to that spread |

## Reordering spreads

Drag a spread thumbnail in the footer to a new position to reorder it. Cover spreads — the first spread, and also the last one when *Cover as front & back pages* is enabled — stay fixed.

## Deleting spreads

Click the **− Spread** button in the footer, or select a spread in the footer and press **Delete**. A confirmation dialog appears. Cover spreads cannot be deleted, and at least one content spread must remain (two if endpapers are enabled). A print-shop preset may require more — see [Exporting to PDF](export-pdf.md#export-options).

## Spread settings

Click on empty canvas (deselecting all frames and text) to show **Spread Settings** in the right sidebar:

| Setting | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Margins** | Top, right, bottom, left insets (mm) applied to all frames on this spread. Can be linked or set per-side. |
| **Left page background** | Background colour for the left page |
| **Right page background** | Background colour for the right page |

## Endpapers

Enable **Endpapers** in Project Settings (gear icon) to designate the first and last spreads as cover / endpaper pages. These spreads are independent of the content spreads and are styled separately. At least two content spreads (plus the cover) are required when endpapers are on; one is added automatically if needed.
