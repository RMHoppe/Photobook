## What is a spread?

A spread is a pair of facing pages (left + right). The canvas always shows one spread at a time. The first and last spreads may be single pages (covers).

## Adding spreads

Click **+ Spread** in the toolbar. A new blank spread is appended at the end of the book.

## Navigating spreads

Use the **← / →** arrow buttons in the footer, or click a spread thumbnail directly.

## Page size and margins

Open **Project Settings** in the right sidebar (click on empty canvas to deselect all). You can set:

- Page width and height in mm
- Default bleed and margin values that apply to new spreads
